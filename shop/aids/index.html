Good to know

  • These are everyday helpers, not medical devices. For anything that is part of a care plan, check with the person’s occupational therapist.
  • Clean with soap and warm water or a disinfecting wipe. Keep them out of the dishwasher and away from heat, which can bend the plastic.
  • Most orders ship in 7 to 10 business days. Large facility orders may take longer; we’ll tell you when we confirm.
