Privacy

Updated November 19, 2026

This page covers travisvought.com. Each of the guides it links to has its own privacy page.

What we count

We count visits with GoatCounter. It uses no cookies and keeps no personal information. It records the page you opened, the site that sent you, your browser and screen size, and a rough country worked out from your internet address. The address itself is not stored.

The short links on this site, like travisvought.com/contact, are counted the same way before they send you on.

What you type

There are no accounts, no sign-up and no forms on this site. Nothing you do here is saved or sent to us.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep server records, including internet addresses, for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Ads and sponsors

Some of the sites linked here may show ads or sponsor spots. Every one is labeled and kept apart from the listings, and no one can pay to change what a guide lists or how it ranks.

If a site uses an ad network such as Google AdSense, that network and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to that site and others. You can turn off personalized ads in Google's ad settings, read how Google uses cookies in ads, or opt out of many networks at aboutads.info.

What we don't do

We don't sell personal information. This site is not meant for children under 13.
