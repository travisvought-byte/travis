# Grants for Real: operational plan

Live at https://travisvought.com/grants/ (self-contained folder with relative links, so it can move to its own repository and domain unchanged). Built by `python3 tools/build_grants.py`; edit that script, not the pages.

## Problem
Small and new organizations lose grants for reasons unrelated to the quality of their work: lapsed status, slow registrations, misread criteria, budgets that don't match the narrative, and advice that is opinion passed off as rule.

## Information needed
1. What funders verify before reading: legal entity, IRS status, current 990s, state charity registration, SAM.gov.
2. What reviewers score: published criteria, merit review, risk review.
3. The money rules: allowable costs, indirect rates, audit and equipment thresholds, record retention.
4. Where opportunities are posted: federal, state (Ohio first), foundations, companies.
5. Who actually gives, and how much.
6. How groups without a 501(c)(3) take part (fiscal sponsorship).
7. Which common beliefs are false.

## Where it lives
| Need | Source |
|---|---|
| Federal rules | 2 CFR 200 (200.205, .206, .334, .403, .414, Appendix I), 2 CFR 25.200, EPA summary of the 2024 revision |
| Registration | Grants.gov organization registration and error pages, SAM.gov |
| IRS status | IRS automatic revocation, 990-N, Tax Exempt Organization Search, private foundation payout pages |
| Ohio | OBM Ohio Grants Partnership policy and job aid (grants.ohio.gov, OH\|ID, Ohio Pays), Ohio Attorney General charity registration |
| Who gives | Giving USA 2026 (2025 giving) |
| Who foundations fund | 990-PF grant lists via ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer; Candid search (free at 2,180+ locations) |
| Ethics | AFP Standard 21; Standards for Excellence |
| Scams | Grants.gov, FTC (March 2026), USAGov |
| Fiscal sponsorship | Nonprofit Law Blog (Takagi, 2025) |

Anything drawn from practitioner judgment instead of a rule or dataset is tagged "Field practice" on the page.

## Gaps
- State pages beyond Ohio (pattern documented on find.html).
- Published success rates by program or funder.
- Fiscal sponsor fee survey data.
- Ohio community foundations by county.
