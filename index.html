Veterans and seniors

  • Veteran Home Guardiansvethomeguard.org An Ohio nonprofit with free guides to veteran benefits, care, housing and support, and paid veteran-to-veteran visits for isolated veterans in nursing homes.
  • Veteran Resident Resource Guideguide.vethomeguard.org Benefits, care, housing and local offices for senior and disabled veterans, state by state. Written to hand to a resident, a family or a nursing home.

Disability and mobility

  • Mobility Talkmobilitytalk.org Help for people with disabilities in every state: benefits, equipment and repairs, home access, rides, in-home help, work, vision and hearing services, and your rights.
  • Mobility Independence Foundationthemif.org A foundation working on independence through mobility. I serve as its Finance Officer and help with planning and operations.

Moms and families

  • Yoga Mom Talkyogamomtalk.com A place for moms to connect, with real help when you need it: food, child care, health coverage, disability help and crisis lines in all 50 states. OhioMom is its Ohio branch. Founded by Deborah Vought.

First Peoples

  • Nina's Mapninas-map.com A free map for First Peoples: Nations, Native clinics, community centers, tribal colleges and tribal lands, with a filter for places that don't ask for a tribal card. Dedicated to Nina, and hers to share.

Bingo players and halls

  • Find Bingofindbingo.org Type a ZIP code and find bingo games near you, state by state. The Ohio finder is at findbingo.org/ohio.
  • Ohio Bingoohiobingo.org Find a game, fill a hall, fund a cause. Search Ohio bingo by ZIP code, day and time. Listings and promotion help are free for halls.

Cannabis information

  • OhioWeedohioweed.org A practical Ohio cannabis guide: a source-linked business directory, licensed dispensary locations, events, and plain-language links to Ohio's rules.
  • The Reeferthe-reefer.com Plain-language cannabis answers for all 50 states and DC: what is legal, where to buy, clearing a record and where to get help, each traced to a named source.

What I provide

The guides are free and stay free. When an organization needs more than a listing, this is the work I take on.

  • Resource guides and directories

    Researched, searchable, and built to hand to the person who needs it. I can build one for a county, a cause or an industry, and set it up to stay current.

  • Compliance and risk review

    Eleven years of anti-money-laundering and know-your-customer work at J.P. Morgan, applied to regulated fields that get little support: charitable gaming, cannabis and small nonprofits.

  • Help for bingo halls

    I ran and promoted bingo at the Marengo American Legion for three years. I help halls bring in more players for less advertising money, and look over operations against Ohio's bingo rules.

  • Nonprofit planning

    Capital plans, funding proposals, sponsor and relationship tracking, and practical use of AI as research support, with people keeping the judgment.

Background

Intelligence work and compliance work come down to one habit: find out what is true, write it down so someone else can check it, and act on it. The guides are that habit pointed at everyday problems.

  1. Growing up

    Upstate New York, Eagle Scout

    Raised around Horseheads and Elmira, one of six kids.

  2. 2003 to 2011

    United States Marine Corps, Staff Sergeant

    Administration, then counterintelligence and human intelligence. Deployed to Fallujah, Iraq in 2007 and to Helmand, Afghanistan in 2009 and 2010 as a regimental intelligence chief.

  3. 2011 to 2012

    Intelligence analyst, Helmand

    A further year in Afghanistan as a civilian contractor.

  4. Eleven years

    J.P. Morgan Asset Management

    Anti-money-laundering and know-your-customer compliance, finishing as a vice president leading risk assessment. Certified Anti-Money Laundering Specialist. Chaired the firm's veterans group for two years and kept its cemetery cleanups going through COVID.

  5. 2025

    Founded Veteran Home Guardians

    An Ohio 501(c)(3) nonprofit built on paid veteran-to-veteran visits in long-term care. It also publishes free resource guides.

  6. 2026

    Finance Officer, Mobility Independence Foundation

    Elected by the board in July.

Introduce me

For podcast hosts, event organizers and anyone passing my name along. Copy the bio, send the page, or print the code.

Travis Vought is a Marine Corps veteran and the founder and chair of Veteran Home Guardians, an Ohio nonprofit. He served eight and a half years in the Marines, including intelligence work in Iraq and Afghanistan, then spent eleven years in anti-money-laundering compliance at J.P. Morgan Asset Management. Today he builds free, searchable guides that help veterans, people with disabilities, moms and local communities find real help close to home. He is also the author of the Alpha to Omega series, which begins with Atlantis to Eden. He lives in Marengo, Ohio.

Get involved

These guides get better the same way a town does: people who know something speak up.

  • Send a correction. A wrong phone number, a new game night, a program that closed. Every guide has a way to report it.
  • Share a guide. Copy a ready reply for someone who asked for help, print a ready-made flyer, or hand it to the one person you know who needs it.
  • List your organization. Listings are free. If you serve the people a guide is for, you belong in it.
  • Sponsor or donate. Support goes to Veteran Home Guardians, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Sponsor a guide or make a donation.
