Find the guide you need

Every site below is free to use. Each one is built from public sources and kept current by the people who use it. If something is wrong, tell me and I fix it.

What I provide

The guides are free and stay free. When an organization needs more than a listing, this is the work I take on.

  • Resource guides and directories

    Researched, searchable and built to be handed to the person who needs them. I can build one for a county, a cause or an industry, and set it up so it stays current.

  • Compliance and risk review

    Eleven years of anti-money-laundering and know-your-customer work, applied to regulated fields that get little support: charitable gaming, cannabis, small nonprofits.

  • Help for bingo halls

    Advertising that costs less and reaches more players, plus a second set of eyes on operations and Ohio's bingo rules. It starts with a conversation.

  • Nonprofit planning

    Capital plans, funding proposals, sponsor and relationship tracking, and practical ways to use AI as research support while people keep the judgment.

Background

Intelligence work and compliance work come down to one habit: find out what is true, write it down so someone else can check it, and act on it. The guides are that habit pointed at everyday problems.

  1. Growing up

    Upstate New York, Eagle Scout

    Raised around Horseheads and Elmira, one of six kids.

  2. 2003 to 2011

    United States Marine Corps, Staff Sergeant

    Administration, then counterintelligence and human intelligence. Deployed to Fallujah, Iraq in 2007 and to Helmand, Afghanistan in 2009 and 2010 as a regimental intelligence chief.

  3. 2011 to 2012

    Intelligence analyst, Helmand

    A further year in Afghanistan as a civilian contractor.

  4. Eleven years

    J.P. Morgan Asset Management

    Anti-money-laundering and know-your-customer compliance, finishing as a vice president leading risk assessment. Certified Anti-Money Laundering Specialist. Chaired the firm's veterans group for two years.

  5. 2025

    Founded Veteran Home Guardians

    An Ohio 501(c)(3) built on paid veteran-to-veteran visits in long-term care. Before that, three years running and promoting bingo at the Marengo American Legion.

  6. 2026

    Finance Officer, Mobility Independence Foundation

    Elected by the board in July, alongside building the guides listed above.

Get involved

These guides get better the same way a town does: people who know something speak up.

  • Send a correction. A wrong phone number, a new game night, a program that closed. Every guide has a way to report it.
  • Share a guide. Print it, post it, or hand it to the one person you know who needs it.
  • List your organization. Listings are free. If you serve the people a guide is for, you belong in it.
  • Partner or sponsor. Donations and sponsorships go to Veteran Home Guardians, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit.
