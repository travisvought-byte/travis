Help someone find help

When someone asks where to turn, you can point them to a free guide in a few seconds. Pick the guide, copy the reply, paste it. Every guide is free, needs no sign-up, and is built from public sources.

  1. Find the guide that fits the person below.
  2. Copy the reply and paste it where they asked, or show them the QR code.
  3. If you know their state or town, add it. A reply that answers their exact question helps most.

  • Mobility Talk

    For: Adults with cannabis questions: what's legal where they live, medical cards, and clearing an old record.

  • OhioWeed

    For: Ohioans with cannabis questions, and Ohio cannabis businesses.

Answering in groups and forums

  • Answer the question first, then share the link. People trust a reply that helps before it points somewhere.
  • Check the group's rules. Many allow helpful links but not repeated posts of the same link.
  • Share the page for their state when you can. Each guide has state pages.
  • If someone is in danger or crisis, point them to 911 or the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline first.

Put the guides on your website

Libraries, county offices, churches, senior centers, schools and nonprofits are welcome to list the guides. Copy this and paste it into your resources page.
